Joule's law

Heat produced in a wire grows with the square of the current, with the resistance and with the time. Double the current and the heat becomes 4 times.

H = I²Rt

Electric power

Power is the energy used in one second. Use the form that matches what you are given.

P = VI = I²R = V²/R

One unit of energy

One unit is one kilowatt hour: a 1000 W appliance running for 1 hour. It is the unit used on your electricity bill.

1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J

Why nichrome?

A heating element needs a high resistivity, a high melting point and no rusting when red hot. Nichrome has all three.

R = ρ l / A

Same voltage, different resistance

At the same voltage a smaller resistance draws more current, so the power is more. A 60 W bulb has a smaller resistance than a 40 W bulb.

P = V²/R

Saving energy

Use LED lamps, BLDC fans and 5-star appliances. Run heavy appliances in the cheaper hours. Solar panels give electricity without pollution.

Energy saved = energy produced
