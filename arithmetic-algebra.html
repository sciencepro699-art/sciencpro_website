KEY IDEAS

Three things to remember

Algebraic form

The number in front of n is the common difference. The number added at the end is the first term minus the common difference.

xₙ = dn + (f − d)

From two terms

Divide the change in value by the change in position to get d. Then put one known term in to find the rest.

d = (xq − xp) ÷ (q − p)

Sum of terms

Add the first and last term, multiply by the number of terms, and halve it.

Sum = (x₁ + xₙ) × n ÷ 2
