KEY IDEAS

The four things to remember

Favourable ÷ possible

Probability is the number of outcomes we want, divided by the number of all possible outcomes. Every outcome must be equally likely.

P = favourable / possible

Always between 0 and 1

An impossible event has probability 0. A sure event has probability 1. Everything else is a fraction in between.

0 ≤ P ≤ 1

Not happening

The chance that something does not happen is what is left after taking its probability away from 1.

P(not E) = 1 − P(E)

Probability from area

When a dot falls at random on a figure, the chance of landing in a part is the area of that part divided by the whole area.

P = part area / whole area
