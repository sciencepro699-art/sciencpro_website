KEY IDEAS

Three rules to remember

Two chords inside

When two chords cross at P inside a circle, the two parts of one chord multiply to the same number as the two parts of the other.

PA × PB = PC × PD

Two secants from outside

Lines from an outside point P that cut the circle twice. Multiply P to the near point, by P to the far point, on each line.

PA × PB = PC × PD

Tangent and secant

The tangent touches the circle at one point T. Its length squared equals the near part times the far part of the secant.

PT² = PA × PB
