Reading a point

Two number lines cross at the origin. A point is written as (x, y): first sideways, then up or down.

(x, y)

Length of a line

The line joining two points is the diagonal of a rectangle. Use Pythagoras on its two sides.

√((x₁ − x₂)² + (y₁ − y₂)²)

Midpoint and ratio

To reach a point on the line, take that fraction of the change in x and of the change in y.

midpoint = ½ of the way

Slope and line

Slope is how much y changes for each step in x. With slope m and a point, you can write the equation of the line.

m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁), y = mx + c

Equation of a circle

Every point on a circle is the same distance r from the centre (a, b). That distance rule gives the equation.

(x − a)² + (y − b)² = r²

Circle on a diameter

The centre is the midpoint of the diameter. The radius is half the diameter. Then use the circle equation.

centre = midpoint of the two ends
