KEY IDEAS

Four things to remember

Dividing a line

The point P that divides AB in the ratio m : n. When m = n, P is the midpoint.

x = x₁ + m/(m+n) × (x₂ − x₁)

Slope

How steep a line is: the rise divided by the run between two points on it.

slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

Circle

A circle with centre (a, b) and radius r. Every point (x, y) on it is r away from the centre.

(x − a)² + (y − b)² = r²

Parallelogram

The diagonals share one midpoint, so the fourth vertex D is found from A, B and C.

D = (x₁ + x₃ − x₂, y₁ + y₃ − y₂)
