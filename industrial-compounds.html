Ammonia

Made in the lab by heating NH₄Cl with Ca(OH)₂. It is a basic gas, very soluble in water, and is dried with quicklime (CaO).

NH₄Cl + Ca(OH)₂ → NH₃

Haber process

Industrial ammonia: nitrogen and hydrogen in the ratio 1 : 3 with an iron catalyst.

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ + heat

Equilibrium

In a reversible reaction products turn back into reactants. At equilibrium the forward and backward rates are equal.

forward rate = backward rate

Le Chatelier's principle

When you disturb an equilibrium, it shifts to cancel the change. More pressure goes to the side with fewer molecules. More heat goes to the side that absorbs heat. Removing a product makes more product.

disturb → it shifts back

Contact process

Sulphuric acid is made from sulphur. SO₂ becomes SO₃ using V₂O₅ at about 450 °C. SO₃ is dissolved in conc. H₂SO₄ and then diluted with water.

2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃

Uses of H₂SO₄

It is a dehydrating agent (sugar turns black), a drying agent and it displaces other acids from salts, such as HCl from NaCl.

king of chemicals

Basicity

The number of H⁺ ions an acid can give per molecule. HCl is 1, H₂SO₄ is 2, H₃PO₄ is 3.

H₂SO₄ is dibasic

Acids and bases

An acid gives H⁺ in water. A base gives OH⁻ in water. NaOH is a strong alkali.

NaOH → Na⁺ + OH⁻

Salt hydrolysis

A salt of a strong acid and a weak base is acidic. A salt of a strong acid and a strong base, like CaCl₂, is neutral.

CaCl₂ → neutral
