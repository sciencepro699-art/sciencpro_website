What to notice

The solutions of the equation are the points where the graph crosses the x-axis.

D = b² − 4ac decides how many solutions there are: D > 0 gives two, D = 0 gives one, D < 0 gives none.

Try changing only c and watch the graph move up and down.

Polynomial and degree

An expression like 3x² + 2x − 5. The highest power of x is its degree. x + 1 is first degree and x² + 3x + 2 is second degree.

degree = highest power of x

Multiplying first degree polynomials

The product of two first degree polynomials is a second degree polynomial.

(x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab

Factors and solutions

To factorise x² + 5x + 6, find two numbers with sum 5 and product 6. A product is zero only if one factor is zero.

(x − 1)(x − 3) = 0 → x = 1 or x = 3

The solution formula

For ax² + bx + c = 0, the solutions are given by one formula.

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a
