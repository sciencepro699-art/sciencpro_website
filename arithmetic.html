KEY IDEAS

Three things to remember

Common difference

The same number is added at every step. Subtract any term from the next one to find it.

d = next term − term

Any term

To reach term n from the first term, make n − 1 jumps of d.

xₙ = f + (n − 1) × d

Adding the terms

The first and last terms, the second and second last, and so on, all add to the same number.

Sum = (first + last) × n ÷ 2
