Convex lens

A lens that is thicker in the middle. It bends parallel rays so they meet at the principal focus F, so it is also called a converging lens.

f = distance from O to F

Lens formula

Relates object distance u, image distance v and focal length f, using the Cartesian sign convention.

1/f = 1/v − 1/u

Magnification

Ratio of image height to object height. A negative value means the image is inverted (and real); a positive value means erect.

m = hᵢ / hₒ = v / u

Real vs virtual

A real image forms on the opposite side and can be caught on a screen. A virtual image forms on the object's side and cannot — like in a magnifying glass.

Inside F → virtual, erect, enlarged
