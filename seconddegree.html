Perfect square

A square of side x + a is made of an x² square, two strips of ax, and a small a² corner.

(x + a)² = x² + 2ax + a²

Completing the square

To make x² + bx a perfect square, add the square of half of b to both sides.

add (b/2)²

Two answers, or none

If (x + p)² = q, then x + p can be +√q or −√q. If q is negative there is no real solution.

x + p = ±√q
