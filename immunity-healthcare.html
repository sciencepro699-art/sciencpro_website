Immunity and Healthcare

How does the body fight germs? Read the key ideas, then test yourself.

Health and antigens

Health is complete physical, mental and social well-being, not only the absence of disease. Anything that enters the body and causes disease is an antigen. Examples: pathogens, pollen, dust, some foods and chemicals. Immunity is the ability to keep pathogens out and destroy those that enter.

immunity: innate + acquired

Innate immunity

It is present from birth and is non-specific. Primary level: wax in the ear, lysozyme in saliva and tears, mucus and cilia, sweat and sebum, HCl in the stomach, useful bacteria. Secondary level: white blood cells, phagocytosis, inflammation, fever, clotting.

primary → secondary

White blood cells

Neutrophil and monocyte: engulf and destroy germs (phagocytes). Eosinophil: acts in allergy and destroys parasites. Basophil: makes heparin (stops clotting) and histamine (inflammation).

phagocytes: neutrophil, monocyte

Inflammation and fever

Injured cells release histamine. Blood vessels widen, more blood and WBCs reach the area. Pyrogens from white blood cells make the brain raise the body temperature. Fever helps phagocytes work faster and slows pathogens.

pyrogens → fever

Clotting and healing

Platelets and damaged tissue make thromboplastin. It changes prothrombin into thrombin. Thrombin changes fibrinogen into fibrin threads. Fibrin traps red blood cells and forms a clot. New tissue then heals the wound, and a scar may remain.

prothrombin → thrombin · fibrinogen → fibrin

Acquired immunity

It develops after birth and is specific. B lymphocytes form and mature in bone marrow and make antibodies. T lymphocytes form in bone marrow, mature in the thymus gland and destroy infected cells and cancer cells.

B: antibodies · T: kill cells

Vaccines

Vaccines act as antigens and make the body produce antibodies in advance. MMR has weakened pathogens. OPV has dead pathogens. HPV vaccine has parts of the pathogen. COVID-19 vaccine has part of the genetic material. Edward Jenner is the father of vaccination.

vaccine = artificial immunity

Blood groups

A has A antigen and b antibody. B has B antigen and a antibody. AB has both antigens and no antibodies. O has no antigens and both antibodies. D antigen (Rh factor) makes a group positive. A wrong match causes agglutination. Bombay blood group is rare.

AB: no antibodies

Spread of disease

Communicable (contagious) diseases spread by contaminated food and water, droplets in the air, direct contact, surfaces and vectors such as mosquitoes. Prevent them with clean water, hygiene, vaccines and by removing mosquito breeding places.

Anopheles: malaria · Culex: filariasis

HIV and AIDS

HIV attacks T lymphocytes, so the body cannot fight other diseases. It spreads by sexual contact, shared needles, infected blood or organs, and from mother to foetus. There is no complete cure. ART controls the virus. Infected people need care, not isolation.

HIV → T lymphocytes

Non-contagious and hereditary

Cancer is uncontrolled cell division that spreads through blood and lymph. Lifestyle diseases: diabetes, hypertension. Also occupational diseases, autoimmune disorders and nutritional disorders. Hereditary diseases: sickle cell anaemia (haemoglobin gene) and haemophilia (clotting protein gene).

not spread person to person

Treatment and antibiotics

Systems: Ayurveda, Homeopathy, Modern medicine. Antibiotics act on bacteria only. Fleming found penicillin in 1928. Wrong use causes diarrhoea, allergy, liver and kidney damage, loss of useful bacteria and antibiotic resistance. Take them only as the doctor says.

antibiotic resistance

Animals and plants

Animal diseases: foot and mouth disease, anthrax, mastitis, Ranikhet. Plant diseases: wilt of brinjal, blight of paddy, bunchy top of banana, tapioca mosaic, bud rot of coconut. Plants defend with a cell wall, waxy coat, antimicrobial substances, enzymes and callose.

callose stops spread
