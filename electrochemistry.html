Electrochemistry

Build cells from different metals, see which metal displaces which, and watch electrolysis and electroplating.

1 · Build a galvanic cell

Pick two metals. The more reactive metal gives up electrons. Watch the electrons flow through the wire and see the voltage.

2 · Displacement reactions

3 · Electrolysis

A battery pushes electricity through a liquid and splits it. Choose an experiment.

Galvanic cell

A galvanic cell changes chemical energy into electrical energy. The Daniell cell has a zinc rod in zinc sulphate and a copper rod in copper sulphate, joined by a salt bridge.

chemical → electrical

Anode and cathode

Oxidation happens at the anode and reduction at the cathode. In a galvanic cell the anode is negative. In an electrolytic cell the anode is positive.

Galvanic: anode − · cathode +

Salt bridge

A tube of an inert electrolyte (KCl, NH₄Cl or KNO₃) joins the two half cells. It completes the circuit and keeps each solution electrically neutral without letting them mix.

completes the circuit

Reactivity series

K, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Ni, Sn, H, Cu, Hg, Ag. A metal higher in the series is more reactive and is oxidised more easily. It displaces a lower metal from its salt solution.

Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu

Oxidising and reducing agents

The oxidising agent causes oxidation and is itself reduced. The reducing agent causes reduction and is itself oxidised. The more reactive metal is the reducing agent.

Mg + Cu²⁺ → Mg²⁺ + Cu

Metals and acids

Metals above hydrogen in the series displace hydrogen from dilute acids. Copper, silver and gold, which are below hydrogen, do not.

Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂

Electrolysis

Electrolysis uses electricity to bring about a chemical change. Molten NaCl gives sodium at the cathode and chlorine at the anode. NaCl solution gives hydrogen at the cathode, chlorine at the anode and NaOH.

molten NaCl → Na + Cl₂

Electroplating

Coating one metal on another by electrolysis. The object to be plated is the cathode (negative). The plating metal is the anode (positive). The solution is a salt of the plating metal.

object = cathode (−)

Types of cells

Primary cells (dry cell, button cell) cannot be recharged. Secondary cells (lead acid, lithium ion) can be recharged. Fuel cells give electricity as long as fuel is supplied.

primary · secondary · fuel
